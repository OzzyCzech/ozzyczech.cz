Quick comparison of **2024–2026** action, pocket, and 360° cameras: sensor size, video highlights, stabilisation type, waterproofing, battery, and weight. Models are listed newest first. Price columns link to live Heureka listings rather than quoting figures that go stale.

:::note
The 2026 line-up shifted significantly. GoPro **skipped the HERO14 Black entirely** and put its next-generation silicon into the **MISSION 1** series instead; Insta360's 360° flagship is now the **X6** and it entered the pocket-gimbal market with the Leica co-engineered **Luna** series; and DJI replaced the Pocket 3 with the **Pocket 4** and **Pocket 4P**.
:::

## GoPro

### [GoPro MISSION 1 series](https://gopro.com/en/us/news/gopro-announces-three-cameras-mission-1-2026) (2026)

GoPro's next-generation platform, pitched as compact **cinema** cameras rather than a HERO refresh. MISSION 1 and MISSION 1 PRO reached retail globally on **28 May 2026**. The interchangeable-lens **PRO ILS** followed: pre-orders opened **26 August 2026** with shipping from **2 September 2026**.

- **Sensor:** 50 MP 1" **quad Bayer**, binning to 12 MP in 4K modes; 1.6 µm native pixel pitch, effective 3.2 µm in 4K; ~14 stops dynamic range at sensor level
- **Processor:** **GP3** on a 5 nm node (GP2 was 12 nm), with an AI NPU for pixel processing and low light
- **Video (PRO):** 8K/60, 4K/240, 1080p/960 (16:9); 8K/30 and 4K/120 **Open Gate** (4:3); 50 MP RAW stills
- **Video (non-PRO):** 8K/30, 4K/120, 1080p/240 (16:9); Open Gate capped at 4K/120
- **Waterproof:** 20 m without a housing, 60 m with the protective housing
- **Battery:** Enduro 2; 5+ h at 1080p30, 3+ h at 4K30 per charge
- **PRO ILS:** GoPro's first interchangeable-lens body — a **Micro Four Thirds** mount with no electronic contacts, so manual-focus / manual-aperture glass only (lenses sold separately); ~2.7× crop vs full frame (~3× with stabilisation). 197 g body, 8K/60, 4K/240 and 1440p/480, Open Gate to 8K/30 and 4K/120, 10-bit **GP-Log2**, four-mic array with 32-bit float audio written to a separate `.wav`
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/)

:::caution
GoPro did **not** release a HERO14 Black. It skipped a HERO flagship in 2025 and has not launched one in 2026 either as of August, moving the GP3 silicon into MISSION 1 instead — the first break in a nearly decade-long annual HERO cadence. The HERO13 Black below remains the current HERO-line camera.
:::

### [GoPro MAX 2](https://gopro.com/en/us/cameras/max-2) (2025)

- **Sensor:** dual 1/2", 29 MP (360° composite)
- **Video:** 8K/30 (360°), single-lens 4K/60
- **Stabilisation:** Max HyperSmooth + 360° Horizon Lock (EIS)
- **Waterproof:** 5 m without a housing
- **Battery:** 1960 mAh Enduro; ~73 min at 8K360/30, ~100 min at 1440p/60
- **Weight:** ~240 g
- **Stand-out features:** Tool-free twist-off replaceable lenses, six mics for 360° audio, dual mode (360° + traditional), “true” 8K 360° pipeline (per GoPro marketing)
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/gopro-max2/)

### [GoPro HERO13 Black](https://gopro.com/en/us/shop/cameras/learn/hero13black/CHDHX-131-master.html) (2024)

- **Sensor:** 1/1.9", 27 MP
- **Video:** 5.3K/60, 4K/120, 2.7K/240; 10-bit HLG HDR; burst slow-mo up to ~400 fps (720p)
- **Stabilisation:** HyperSmooth 6.0 with AutoBoost (digital / EIS)
- **Waterproof:** 10 m without a housing
- **Battery:** 1900 mAh Enduro; ~150 min at 4K30, ~90 min at 5.3K30
- **Weight:** 154 g (with battery and mounting fingers)
- **Stand-out features:** Modular **HB-Series** lenses (ultra-wide, anamorphic, macro), Wi-Fi 6, GPS overlay, live streaming, webcam mode
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/gopro-hero13-black/)

## DJI

### [DJI Osmo Pocket 4P](https://www.dji.com/osmo-pocket-4p) (2026)

Dual-lens variant, launched globally **30 July 2026**. DJI's own name is **Pocket 4P**; some coverage calls it "Pocket 4 Pro".

- **Sensor / optics:** dual-lens setup on a 1" sensor, Hasselblad colour science
- **Video:** 10-bit **D-Log 2**
- **Weight:** 230 g
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/)

### [DJI Osmo Pocket 4](https://www.dji.com/osmo-pocket-4) (2026)

Announced in China on 26 March 2026 and launched globally on **16 April 2026**, replacing the Pocket 3 as DJI's gimbal-in-pocket camera.

- **Sensor:** new 1" CMOS with dual conversion gain, ~20 mm equiv., **f/2.0**; ~14 stops dynamic range; measured ISO 100–12 800 (Pocket 3: 100–6 400)
- **Video:** 4K up to 60 standard, **4K/240** slow-mo; 10-bit D-Log
- **Stills:** 37 MP (7680 × 4320, 16:9) with DNG RAW
- **Stabilisation:** 3-axis mechanical gimbal, as on Pocket 3
- **Battery:** 1545 mAh (~20% larger than Pocket 3); up to ~4 h at 1080p
- **Storage:** **107 GB built in** at ~800 MB/s — no card needed for most shoots
- **Stand-out features:** Larger touchscreen, built-in storage, markedly lighter body than Pocket 3
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/)

:::note
Neither the Pocket 4 nor the Pocket 4P launched in the **United States** — DJI shipped both globally while excluding the US market. Availability in the EU, including Czechia, is unaffected.
:::

### [DJI Osmo Action 6](https://www.dji.com/osmo-action-6) (2025) — current DJI action flagship

Launched globally **18 November 2025** and still DJI's top action camera as of August 2026.

- **Sensor:** 1/1.1" CMOS, 38 MP, ~13.5 stops; ~2.4 µm pixel pitch
- **Video:** 4K/120, 8K stills; variable aperture **f/2.0–f/4.0**
- **Stabilisation:** RockSteady + HorizonSteady (EIS); HorizonSteady in 4K (generation-dependent — verify firmware notes)
- **Waterproof:** IP68; 20 m bare, 60 m with Dive Case
- **Battery:** 1950 mAh Battery Plus; ~240 min (1080p), ~127 min (4K60); fast charge claims (~2 h runtime from ~15 min charge, per DJI marketing)
- **Weight:** 149 g
- **Stand-out features:** Square (4:3-native) sensor, variable aperture, **50 GB** internal storage, swappable lens options, dual OLED
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/dji-osmo-action-6-standard-combo-cp-os-00000505_03/)

### [DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro](https://www.dji.com/osmo-action-5-pro) (2024)

- **Sensor:** 1/1.3" CMOS, 40 MP, ~13.5 stops advertised dynamic range
- **Video:** 4K/120, 2.7K/120, 1080p/240
- **Stabilisation:** RockSteady 3.0 + HorizonSteady (EIS)
- **Waterproof:** IP68; 20 m bare, 60 m with Waterproof Case
- **Battery:** 1950 mAh Extreme Battery Plus; ~216 min (4K30), up to ~4 h at lower resolutions; rated down to **−20 °C**
- **Weight:** 146 g
- **Stand-out features:** Dual OLED screens, built-in pressure sensor (depth), subject tracking, cold-weather battery behaviour
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/dji-osmo-action-5-pro-standard-combo-cp-os-00000349_01/)

### [DJI Osmo Pocket 3](https://www.dji.com/cz/osmo-pocket-3) (2023) — previous generation

- **Sensor:** 1" CMOS, 20 MP; **f/2.0**, ~20 mm equiv.
- **Video:** 4K/60 standard, 4K/120 slow-mo, 10-bit D-Log M
- **Stabilisation:** **3-axis mechanical gimbal** (not EIS-only)
- **Waterproof:** not submersible; dust resistance only — use a dive case for water work
- **Battery:** 1300 mAh; ~166 min (1080p24), ~116 min (4K60); fast top-up (~32 min to high charge, per DJI)
- **Weight:** 179 g
- **Stand-out features:** Gimbal in pocket form, 2" rotating touchscreen (vertical/horizontal), face/object tracking, strong low-light for the size — common **vlogging** pick
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/dji-osmo-pocket-3-cp-os-00000301_01/)

## Insta360

### [Insta360 X6](https://www.insta360.com/product/insta360-x6) (2026) — current 360° flagship

On sale from **12 August 2026**, superseding the X5.

- **Sensor:** custom **dual Sony 1/1.1" square** sensors — ~33% more sensor area than X5 and roughly 4× the light intake per frame
- **Video:** 8K 360° at 7680 × 3840 up to **50 fps** (also 48/30/25/24); 5K/60 single-lens
- **Colour:** first Insta360 360° camera with **native in-camera Dolby Vision**; 10-bit in 360° mode, plus I-Log
- **Processing:** triple-chip design — one 8-core 3.3 GHz 4 nm processor plus two dedicated imaging chips; ~500% more compute than X5, 106% faster clock, ~35% lower power draw
- **Stabilisation:** FlowState + 360° Horizon Lock (EIS)
- **Waterproof:** IP68, 20 m bare; 60 m with the Invisible Dive Case Pro
- **Battery:** 2600 mAh Xtreme Battery; ~140 min of 8K/30 360° recording
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/)

### [Insta360 Luna Ultra](https://www.insta360.com/product/insta360-luna-ultra) (2026)

Insta360's first **pocket gimbal** camera and its first body **co-engineered with Leica** — a direct answer to the DJI Pocket line. Announced at NAB 2026 and unveiled at Leica's headquarters in Wetzlar; on sale from **10 June 2026**.

- **Main camera:** 1" 8K sensor behind a **Leica Summicron** lens, **f/1.8**; up to ~14 stops dynamic range
- **Telephoto camera:** second 1/1.3" sensor at **f/2.0** — five focal lengths and up to **12× zoom** (6× lossless)
- **Video:** 8K/30 with **Dolby Vision**, 10-bit **I-Log**; PureVideo mode does 4K/60 with noise reduction for low light
- **Stills:** 37 MP UltraPhotos, 200 MP Scenic Panorama
- **Stabilisation:** **3-axis mechanical gimbal** plus EIS; Deep Track 5.0 with auto-tracking, active zoom tracking, group tracking and smart framing
- **Battery:** 1550 mAh, up to ~4 h; ~80% charge in ~23 min
- **Storage:** 47 GB internal plus microSD up to 1 TB
- **Weight:** just over 200 g
- **Stand-out features:** **Modular** body with detachable camera unit and battery, detachable 2" OLED touchscreen with HD transmission up to ~20 m; Cosmic Black and Stellar White finishes
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/)

### Insta360 Luna Pro (2026)

Single-camera sibling of the Luna Ultra, announced alongside it at NAB 2026.

- **Sensor:** 1"
- **Video:** 8K horizontal, **4K vertical**; 10-bit I-Log, Dolby Vision HDR
- **Zoom:** up to 4× digital (~80 mm equivalent)
- **Processing:** dual AI chips for low light and subject tracking; Leica colour profiles
- **Screen:** detachable 2" OLED touchscreen with ~20 m remote range

:::caution
As of August 2026 the Luna Pro is sold **in China only**, with no international pricing or release date announced. The 4K vertical mode is its notable edge over the Pocket 4P, which caps vertical capture lower.
:::

### [Insta360 X5](https://www.insta360.com/product/insta360-x5) (2025) — previous generation

- **Sensor:** dual 1/1.28" CMOS, 72 MP 360° stills, ~13.5 stops
- **Video:** 8K/30 360° (marketing stack from ~11K internal processing), 5.7K/60
- **Stabilisation:** FlowState + 360° Horizon Lock (EIS)
- **Waterproof:** 15 m bare (claimed improvement vs X4), 60 m with Invisible Dive Case
- **Battery:** 2400 mAh; ~88 min active 360° recording / ~180 min total scenarios; +31% vs X4 (per Insta360)
- **Weight:** 200 g
- **Stand-out features:** Replaceable lens guards, invisible selfie-stick workflow, triple AI chip marketing — flagship **consumer 360°** tier in 2025
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/insta360-x5_2/)

### [Insta360 Ace Pro 2](https://www.insta360.com/product/insta360-ace-pro2) (2024)

- **Sensor:** 1/1.3", 50 MP (Leica co-branded), ~13.5 stops
- **Video:** 8K/30, 4K/120, 4K/60 Active HDR
- **Stabilisation:** FlowState + 360° Horizon Lock (EIS)
- **Waterproof:** 12 m without a housing
- **Battery:** 1800 mAh; ~180 min; fast charge to ~80% in ~18 min (per Insta360)
- **Weight:** 177 g
- **Stand-out features:** Leica-tuned optics, dual AI chip story, flip touchscreen, wide FOV options — “pro” non-360 line
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/insta360-ace-pro-2/)

### [Insta360 GO 3S](https://www.insta360.com/product/insta360-go3s) (2024)

- **Sensor:** 1/2", 11 MP (12 MP stills); **f/2.8**
- **Video:** 4K/30, 2.7K/50, 1080p/200 slow-mo; up to ~120 Mbps
- **Stabilisation:** FlowState (EIS)
- **Waterproof:** camera **IPX8** to ~10 m; Action Pod **IPX4** only
- **Battery:** 310 mAh (camera) ~32 min solo; ~122 min with Action Pod at 4K30
- **Weight:** 39 g camera; ~96 g with Action Pod
- **Stand-out features:** Tiny 4K wearable, magnetic mounts, Action Pod with 2.2" flip screen, Apple Find My, pet POV use-cases
- **Price (CZ):** [current listings on Heureka](https://sportovni-kamery.heureka.cz/insta360-go-3s-64gb/) (64 GB)

The H1 2026 window that earlier rumours pointed to passed without a launch, and [reporting now points to late 2026 or early 2027](https://www.dailycameranews.com/2026/08/insta360-ace-pro-3-rumors/).
